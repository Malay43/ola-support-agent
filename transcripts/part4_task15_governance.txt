Risk classification: Medium
I classify this system as MEDIUM risk. It is a customer-support assistant that answers policy questions and reads ticket status, which the scheme lists under 'customer support tickets' (Medium), not under summarisation (Low). It is not High: it handles no medical data, makes no hiring decision and touches no financial account - it cannot issue refunds or change a ticket, it only reads one record and quotes policy.
